Video Case Study Retention & Viral Reach

Scaling Creator Reach with 3-Second Hook Retention

Challenge: The creator had great technical knowledge, but average watch duration dropped after 4 seconds due to static framing.
Solution: We introduced kinetic typography, sound effects every 2.5 seconds, punchy B-roll zooms, and an open-loop curiosity hook in the opening 2 seconds.
Results: Retention spiked to 84% through the first 30 seconds, generating 450,000+ views on Instagram and +6,400 new followers.

Meta Ads Case Study E-Commerce Scale

4.2x ROAS Scaling for Direct-to-Consumer Brand

Challenge: E-commerce brand struggling with 1.4x ROAS and rising customer acquisition costs on Facebook.
Solution: Restructured campaign architecture into Prospecting (Broad + Lookalikes) and Dynamic Retargeting. Tested 4 distinct UGC video angles with clear pain-point demonstrations.
Results: Blended ROAS scaled from 1.4x to 4.2x within 30 days, generating over $24,000 in profitable revenue with lower CPMs.
